    What happens to your answer.

    Every answer goes through the same five steps. Scroll to follow one through.

    1

    A question written for you

    Gemini reads your résumé and your answers so far, then writes one question. With it comes a model answer and the key ideas a good answer should cover.

    2

    You answer in your own words

    Type or speak. There is no keyword list to hit, so explain it the way you would to an interviewer.

    3

    Each idea is checked for meaning

    An embedding model compares every sentence you wrote with every key idea. A correct idea in different words still counts.

    4

    Gemini reads it like an interviewer

    It checks the facts, flags anything stated incorrectly, and quotes your own sentence next to the correction.

    5

    Two scores become one grade

    The judge counts for 60% and the embedding model for 40%. How closely they agree sets the confidence you see.

    Question 3 of 5, Operating Systems

    What is the difference between a process and a thread, and when would you use threads?

    Separate vs shared memoryContext-switch costSynchronisation risk

    A process has its own address space, while threads of the same process share memory. Switching between threads is cheaper. Threads never need locks because they share memory.
